## ☕ Coffee Sales Analytics Dashboard

**A Data‑Driven Exploration of Global Coffee Sales (2019–2022)**
This project analyzes four years of coffee sales data across three countries (**United Kingdom, United States, and Ireland**), customer segments, and coffee bean types. Using Excel, the dashboard uncovers trends in revenue, customer behavior, and product performance to support strategic decision‑making for a coffee retail business.
This project and its data was taken from MO_Chan's Youtube learning platform. Find the link below: [coffee_sales](https://youtu.be/m13o5aqeCbM?si=U0fP9I9c3C-LeXOm)

📌 **Key Insights**

**1. Robusta and Liberica Are the Revenue Powerhouses**
Across 2019–2022, Robusta and Liberica consistently generate the highest monthly sales, often outperforming Arabica and Excelsa by large margins.
    A typical example from the dataset is:
    *Liberica recorded monthly sales as high as $843.71 in Jan 2022*,
    *Robusta peaked at $492.90 in Sep 2019*

**These two bean types dominate due to strong demand and higher‑volume purchases**.

**2. The United States Drives the Majority of Global Revenue**
Country‑level sales show a clear imbalance in market contribution:

*United States: $35,638.88*

*Ireland: $6,696.86*

*United Kingdom: $2,798.50*

**The U.S. accounts for over 75% of all revenue, making it the primary market for expansion, loyalty programs, and targeted promotions**.

**3. Customer Spending Is Highly Fragmented**
The top 5 customers contribute only a small fraction of total revenue:

 With *Allis Wilmore  having the highest contribution of $317.07, and Don Flintiff being the lowest of the top five with a total contribution of $278.01*.

**This indicates a broad customer base with no over‑reliance on a few buyers, which is healthy for long‑term stability**.

**4. Seasonal Patterns Show Strong Mid‑Year Sales**
Across multiple years, June–September consistently show higher sales volumes, especially for Robusta and Liberica.
This suggests that Summer promotions, Cold brew product lines, and Outdoor events, could further boost revenue.
